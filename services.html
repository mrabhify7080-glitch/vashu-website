Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we book for live events?

We recommend booking 4–8 weeks in advance for weddings and major festivals to ensure date availability and custom rehearsal schedules.

Do you travel for outstation and international concerts?

Yes, Vashu and the band perform across all major Indian cities and international venues. Travel and hospitality logistics are coordinated seamlessly.

What is the turnaround time for custom studio music production?

Standard commercial jingles and single track arrangements typically take 7–14 days, including iterative feedback and final mastering.
