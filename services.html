Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking Vashu for your upcoming event.

1. How far in advance should I book Vashu for a live event? +
We recommend booking at least 4 to 8 weeks in advance for destination weddings and corporate galas to ensure date availability and adequate rehearsal time for custom setlists. Urgent bookings can also be accommodated based on calendar availability.
2. What technical equipment (Sound/PA) does the event organizer need to provide? +
For local shows, we can bring complete portable acoustic sound systems. For outstation or large stage venues, we provide a standard Tech Rider (covering mixer channels, IEM monitors, wireless vocal mics, and DI boxes) for your sound vendor to fulfill.
3. Can Vashu perform custom song requests or specific entry tracks? +
Yes! Vashu welcomes custom song requests. For weddings and special galas, we prepare up to 3 dedicated custom songs (such as bride/groom entry or anniversary medleys) when finalized 2 weeks prior to the event date.
4. Is Vashu available for travel across India and internationally? +
Yes, Vashu performs nationwide and internationally. Travel (flight/train tickets) and stay accommodations for Vashu and the band/crew are handled by the event organizer or billed as part of the booking package.
5. What is the standard duration of a live performance? +
A standard live performance set ranges from 90 minutes to 150 minutes (2.5 hours), split into soulful acoustic segments and high-energy dance/pop climaxes as required by the event flow.
6. What is the booking confirmation and payment policy? +
Bookings are officially confirmed upon receipt of a 50% advance booking deposit. The remaining 50% balance is payable on or before the event date prior to soundcheck.
