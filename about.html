Biography & Story

Vashu: The Voice Behind TheMeloFY

Singer, composer, and producer blending the depth of classical Indian melody with the energy of contemporary world soundscapes.

Rooted in Tradition, Crafted for Tomorrow

Music has been my language of expression for over a decade. Starting with classical vocal training and folk roots, my artistic journey expanded into sound design, studio orchestration, and high-energy live performances.

Under the artist identity of TheMeloFY, I produce original tracks, compose scores for digital media and films, and perform live for audiences who seek music that touches the spirit.
