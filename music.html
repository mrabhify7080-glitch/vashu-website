Discography & Releases

Original Music

Every track tells a story. Immerse yourself in Vashu's signature soundscapes across genres.

Sufi Pop Fusion Released: Jan 2026

Ruhaniyat (रूहानियत)

Composed late at night in Vashu's Banaras studio, Ruhaniyat explores spiritual longing and unrequited soul connection. Melding traditional harmonium notes with ambient pad textures and soaring vocal alaps, this track has become a fan-favorite for quiet reflection.

Indie Folk Fusion Released: Nov 2025

Banaras Beats (बनारस बीट्स)

A tribute to Vashu's hometown, combining rhythm patterns sampled directly from the Assi Ghat morning aarti with acoustic guitar arpeggios and modern basslines. Banaras Beats brings the mystic energy of the ancient city into contemporary streaming playlists.

▶ YouTube ◎ Instagram
Acoustic Pop Romance Released: Aug 2025

Pehli Baarish (पहली बारिश)

A warm acoustic ballade evoking the smell of petrichor and nostalgic first rain moments. Featuring layered string harmonies, subtle percussive shakers, and intimate vocal delivery, Pehli Baarish has resonated strongly with lovers of romantic unplugged melodies.

▶ YouTube ◎ Instagram
Rock & Metal Anthem Released: May 2025

Aagahi (आगाही - The Awakening)

Demonstrating Vashu's vocal power and range, Aagahi is a heavy rock anthem driven by distorted guitar riffs, thunderous drum fills, and soaring high octave belts. Written as a call for self-realization and overcoming inner struggles.

▶ YouTube ◎ Instagram
Bollywood Reimagined Released: Mar 2025

Tum Ho Pass (Acoustic Redux)

A soulful acoustic cover and re-imagination of iconic Bollywood melodies, crafted with nylon string guitars, ambient violin overlays, and soft falsetto vocals. A staple highlight of Vashu's unplugged live show setlists.
