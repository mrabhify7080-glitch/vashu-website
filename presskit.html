📌 Short Bio (50 Words — Event Brochures & Socials)

Vashu is an independent singer, composer, songwriter, and music producer from Varanasi, India. Blending classical Hindustani roots, Sufi soul, and modern pop-rock arrangements, Vashu crafts unforgettable live stage experiences and cinematic soundscapes under his artist brand, TheMeloFY.

📌 Medium Bio (150 Words — Festival Programs & Press Features)

Hailing from the spiritual banks of the Ganges in Varanasi, Vashu is a versatile independent musician, vocal powerhouse, and music producer. Over the past six years, he has mastered a 360° independent workflow — writing, composing, singing, and producing original tracks across Sufi fusion, acoustic romance, and energetic pop-rock. Vashu has performed over 100 live shows across India, headline-acting at destination weddings, corporate galas, and youth cultural festivals. Known for his captivating stage presence and emotive vocal improvisations, Vashu continues to redefine modern Indian indie soundscapes through his creative label, TheMeloFY.
