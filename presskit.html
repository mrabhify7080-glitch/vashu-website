Approved Artist Bio

Short Bio (100 Words)

Vashu (TheMeloFY) is an Indian singer, composer, and music producer whose signature sound fuses rich traditional Sufi sensibilities with modern cinematic acoustic orchestration. Known for his captivating live concert presence and pristine studio productions, Vashu has performed at over 50 major music festivals and private cultural galas nationwide. Under his artist imprint TheMeloFY, he crafts original soundtracks, independent singles, and custom scores.

Quick Facts & Highlights

  • Primary Genres: Sufi Pop Fusion, Indie Folk, Cinematic Soundtrack, Semi-Classical
  • Live Formats: Solo Acoustic, 4-Piece Semi-Acoustic, Full 7-Piece Festival Band
  • Location & Base: India (Available for Global Touring)
